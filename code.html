About Tonight's Journey

Perched above the obsidian waves of the North Atlantic, Whispering Cape has stood guard since 1888. Tonight, we follow Arthur, the final lighthouse keeper, as he tends to the brass clockwork lantern under the canopy of an unbroken starry ceiling.

As midnight slips into the pre-dawn stillness of 03:00 AM, the wind settles into a soft rhythm against the stone walls. In this chapter, Arthur tunes into a phantom Morse broadcast from sixty miles across the fjord—not a signal of emergency, but an antique astronomical almanac reading star positions to ships adrift in the dark.
